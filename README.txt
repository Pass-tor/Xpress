XPRESS BOOKING TRACKER - PWA (installable app)

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (free, Netlify Drop - easiest):
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop on your phone or computer.
2. Drag and drop this whole folder (or unzip first, then drop the folder).
3. Netlify gives you a link (https://something.netlify.app).
4. Open the link in Chrome on your Android phone.
5. Tap the menu (3 dots) > "Install app" (or "Add to Home Screen").

Alternative: GitHub Pages - upload all files to a repo, enable Pages.

MOVING YOUR OLD DATA:
Open the old tracker > Backup > Export. Open the new installed app > Backup > Import.
